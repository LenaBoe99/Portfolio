# Portfolio
This repository collects selected bioinformatics projects and analysis from my research and training. 

## Contents 
- **single cell RNAseq:** Analysis of a public scRNAseq dataset using Seurat Workflow, practical of my master programm
- **Spatial transcriptomics:** During a lab rotation in Prof. Benedict Brors Applied Bioinformatics Group at DKFZ, I worked with Xenium spatial transcriptomics data from brain metastatis. I analyzed this single-cell resolution data using R, adapting the workflow from the Seurat vignettes.
- **RNA-seq analysis:** bulk RNAseq workflow, part of my Master Thesis ([knitted script](https://lenaboe99.github.io/Portfolio/Bulk%20RNAseq%20analysis%20-%20Master%20Thesis/bulkRNAseq_CD8+Tcells_anti-CD47.html)) at the Cardio-Immunology Group of PD Dr. Kai-Uwe Jarr at the University Hospital Heidelberg

## Ongoing Projects

I am currently working as research associate at the [MOLIT Institute for personalized medicine](https://www.molit.eu). To better understand drug reponse and recurrance in cancer, the overarching aim of the group is to integrate omics data, functional assays and patient metadata. Currently I am analyzing bulk RNAseq data from colorectal cancer patient derived organoids. We aim to generate more multimodal data to get a deeper insight into the underlying biology of patient response.

I am curious about exploring further dataset and continue to develop my analytical and bioinformatic skills.
